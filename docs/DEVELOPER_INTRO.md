Project Overview

This project is a space agency management simulation that combines the long-term progression of a grand strategy game, the engineering spirit of Kerbal Space Program, and the organisational depth of sports management titles such as Football Manager.

The player does not control spacecraft directly.

Instead, the player acts as the executive director of a space agency belonging to a procedurally generated alien civilisation.

The core fantasy is not "flying rockets."

The core fantasy is "running NASA."

Pillars
1. People Management

The agency is built from individuals.

Scientists have specialties.

Engineers have strengths and weaknesses.

Astronauts develop experience, reputations, and relationships.

Managers influence efficiency and morale.

A player's greatest asset is not technology but talent.

2. Programme Management

The game revolves around programmes rather than missions.

Examples:

Orbital Exploration Programme
Moon Landing Initiative
Deep Space Probe Programme
Military Reconnaissance Programme
Commercial Launch Division

Each programme contains budgets, personnel, objectives, schedules, and risks.

3. Emergent Space History

Players should create stories rather than solve puzzles.

Examples:

A beloved astronaut becomes the first being on another world.
A disastrous launch delays exploration by decades.
A rival nation reaches orbit first.
A cheap probe uncovers evidence of alien ruins.
A budget crisis cancels a flagship mission.

The resulting narrative should feel unique every playthrough.

4. Technological Progression

Technology should emerge from investment and expertise.

Instead of a simple tech tree, progress comes from:

Research funding
Staff experience
Testing campaigns
Mission successes
Industrial capacity

Failures should teach lessons and contribute to future success.

5. Strategic Decision Making

Players constantly balance competing goals:

Safety versus speed
Science versus prestige
Exploration versus profit
Ambition versus reliability
Long-term vision versus political pressure

No decision should have an objectively perfect outcome.

Core Gameplay Loop
Secure funding and political support.
Hire and manage staff.
Invest in research and infrastructure.
Design mission programmes.
Build and launch missions.
Analyse results and gain knowledge.
Expand capabilities.
Repeat at a larger scale.
Long-Term Progression

The campaign starts before the first successful launch.

The player gradually advances through:

Atmospheric research
Orbital flight
Satellites
Lunar exploration
Planetary exploration
Space stations
Resource extraction
Interplanetary transport
Megastructures
Interstellar missions

A full campaign should feel like guiding an entire civilisation through its space age.

The Goal

We are not building a rocket simulator.

We are not building a spreadsheet simulator.

We are building a civilisation-scale management game where players experience the triumphs, disasters, politics, and human stories behind a species' journey from its homeworld to the stars.

The player should finish a campaign feeling that they didn't merely launch rockets.

They built the agency that changed history.
