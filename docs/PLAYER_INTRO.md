Welcome, Director

You have been appointed Director of the Stellar Expansion Authority, the most ambitious organisation in the history of your civilisation.

For centuries, our people studied the heavens from afar. Astronomers mapped distant planets. Scientists debated the origins of the universe. Philosophers wondered whether other minds existed among the stars.

Today, curiosity ends and exploration begins.

Your agency has been tasked with leading our species into space.

You will recruit scientists, engineers, astronauts, administrators, and visionaries. You will manage budgets, appease politicians, oversee research programmes, and make difficult decisions under constant public scrutiny.

Every mission carries risk.

A failed launch can destroy years of work and cripple public confidence. A successful mission can inspire an entire generation and unlock new resources for the future.

Your journey begins with primitive rockets and limited technology. Over time you may establish orbital infrastructure, explore neighbouring worlds, create interplanetary industries, and perhaps one day reach the stars themselves.

There is no single path to success.

Will your agency become a peaceful scientific institution?

A commercial powerhouse?

A military space command?

A pioneering force that pushes the bounds of knowledge regardless of cost?

The future of your civilisation lies in your hands.

Build your agency.

Design your missions.

Make history.

The space age begins now.
